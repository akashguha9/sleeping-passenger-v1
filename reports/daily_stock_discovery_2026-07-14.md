## Scoring And Normalization

The 100-point score uses the requested weights in this order: Structural Node 10, Sovereignty 9, Promotion Geometry 11, Fundamentals 12, Cash/Balance Sheet 8, Valuation 10, Catalysts 8, Entry 7, Poker EV 7, Mines Survival 7, Half-Life 5, Clairefontaine Cohort 4, and Fallacy Protection 2.

India and the US were screened independently with equal 15-name primary capacity. Within-country sector/size percentiles were used for 35% of the comparative assessment and absolute quality for 65%. Valuation is judged against local rates, history, and sector peers. India promoter/pledge/RPT risks are mapped to the same penalty logic as US SBC/dilution/dual-class/customer-concentration risks. The combined board is formed only after those independent screens.

Confidence is separate from opportunity quality and reflects freshness, source hierarchy, evidence agreement, transparency, and forecast uncertainty.

## 12. False-Pattern And Hype Traps

### India

- **"Every EMS/OSAT company is India's TSMC."** Narrative copy. Process IP, yields, customer trust, and foundry scale are absent from most assembly stories.
- **"Defence order approval equals revenue and FCF."** Weak analogy. AoN/order books can take years; milestone billing, imports, and receivables decide conversion.
- **"Every solar manufacturer wins from energy transition."** Survivorship bias. Module pricing, utilization, debt, and Chinese supply determine returns.
- **"Demat growth makes every broker/depository a House."** Structural pattern with an omitted variable: SEBI controls product and fee economics.
- **"7.7% GDP guarantees small-cap returns."** Gambler's fallacy. Small-cap P/E remains above its 10-year median.
- **"Fallen IT is automatically value."** A decline is not a catalyst; bookings, pricing, margin, and FCF must demonstrate conversion.
- **"PSU rerating permanently fixes governance."** Narrative copy. Policy and minority-capital-allocation risks remain company specific.

### United States

- **"Every AI supplier is the next Nvidia."** Weak analogy. Customer concentration, capital intensity, standards risk, and low sovereignty differ.
- **"The memory shortage is permanently secular."** Survivorship bias. Memory remains cyclical even when AI lifts the current demand peak.
- **"AI power demand makes every nuclear/utility stock a compounder."** Separate signed contracts from demand forecasts; permitting, interconnection, capex, and regulation are mines.
- **"Rocket Lab is the next SpaceX."** Narrative copy. Launch cadence, Starlink cash flows, and capital access are not transferable.
- **"Stablecoins kill Visa/Mastercard, so every crypto issuer is a House."** False binary. Distribution, merchant acceptance, reserve income, liquidity, and regulation differ.
- **"A 70% drawdown means a rebound is due."** Gambler's fallacy. Only balance sheet, cash runway, and operating inflection change expected value.
- **"Defence budget growth guarantees contractor margins."** Weak analogy. Fixed-price programmes, labor, and procurement timing can consume headline backlog.
