## SECTION 1 - Daily Global Market Map

Strongest geographies today:
- North Asia remains the strongest proof zone for AI hardware conversion, especially Taiwan, Korea, and Japan, but several names now carry high short-term mine density after parabolic moves.
- India has improved tactically because crude/yield anxiety has eased and domestic liquidity remains supportive, but marketwide FPI selling is still a live mine.
- Europe looks better on risk-adjusted valuation than the US mega-cap AI complex, especially exchanges, electrification, automation, compliance data, and defense rails.
- Latin America and Middle East exposure should be selective: exchanges, airports, banks, and energy cashflow rails look better than broad beta.

Improving odds:
- Exchanges, clearing, depositories, market data, and capital-market plumbing.
- Grid, power equipment, cables, HVDC, data-center power/cooling, and industrial automation.
- Advanced packaging, HBM/memory, IC substrates, OSAT, and semi equipment, but only where proof is already in revenues/margins.
- Hospitals, diagnostics, transaction banking, and professional workflow data.

Overhyped:
- Any stock treated as "AI winner" without booked revenue, cash conversion, or customer dependency proof.
- Indian defense/rail/shipyard baskets where valuation assumes perfect order conversion.
- Hydrogen, EV loss-makers, quantum small caps, crypto-treasury equities, and data-center land-bank stories without funded power.

Liquidity flow:
- Into Asia AI hardware, India domestic-institutional favorites, European value/quality, and exchange/market-infrastructure toll booths.
- Away from fragile EM small caps, Indonesia broad beta, and companies needing repeated funding rounds.

Hidden rails emerging:
- Depositories, RTAs, exchanges, payment licensing, identity/credit data, clearing, HVDC/grid equipment, data-center power and cooling, ports, airports, and rail logistics.

Mines increasing:
- Valuation compression, AI supply-cycle reversal, customer concentration, export controls, India FPI outflows, Middle East oil/geopolitical sensitivity, FX pressure, PSU/regulatory dependence, and promoter/governance risk in smaller India names.
